Region acronyms for engram & morphine work

Every acronym below was validated against allen_mouse_10um_java-Ontology.json in your M3 project — 1,327 atlas structures, filtered to the 138 most relevant to engram tagging and opioid circuitry. Names are the atlas's own, verbatim.

The acronym must match exactly — a typo returns nothing, not an error

Region lookup is an exact string match against the atlas. Ca1, ca1 and CA-1 all silently yield an empty region rather than failing. Click any acronym to copy it.

Watch the habenula/hypothalamus collision: LH is the lateral habenula; the lateral hypothalamic area is LHA. Likewise PL is prelimbic cortex, and LA is the lateral amygdala — not a thalamic nucleus.

How to use these. Put one acronym per line in <project>/regions_of_interest.txt (# comments allowed), or pass them as the regions list in a notebook PARAMS dict. Omit the file entirely and the readout falls back to every included leaf region.

“In your data” means the region carries at least one detected cell somewhere in the M3 7-scene series (73 of 138 do). That series spans rostral forebrain through hippocampus, thalamus and hypothalamus — midbrain, pons and medulla regions are listed for completeness but need a section series that reaches them.

A region can also read as empty when its cells sit on a coarser parent annotation instead of the region itself — the rollup warns by name when that happens.
