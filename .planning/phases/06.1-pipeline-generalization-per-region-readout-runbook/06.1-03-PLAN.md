<objective>
Build the single config-driven consolidated export (D-07) — `scripts/03_export_region_table.groovy` — that fuses the two donor scripts: the all-region loop + config_tag + growing-CSV append pattern from `export_region_dapi_reference.groovy`, and the CR-01 geometric leaf resolution + per-cell TSV + per-entry non-clobbering stem from `03_export_val01_metrics.groovy`. This is the readout deliverable of the phase (PIPE-03/PIPE-04). It takes over the numbered "03 = export" slot; the old `03_export_val01_metrics.groovy` and `export_region_dapi_reference.groovy` are archived in 06.1-04 once this exists.

Purpose: one region-agnostic export producing the per-slice/per-region spine that Phase 10 (AGG-01, EXP-03/04) consumes.
Output: `scripts/03_export_region_table.groovy` (+ deploy copies).
</objective>
