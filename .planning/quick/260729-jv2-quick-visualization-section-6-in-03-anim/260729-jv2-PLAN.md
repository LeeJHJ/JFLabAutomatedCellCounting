**3. `_k_split_counts(project_dir, config, k_values, regions) -> pd.DataFrame`.**

  -- config-derived, intersected with what the export actually carries.

  **LOCKED CORRECTNESS RULE:** the cut applied inside a region is the SECTION-level
  threshold at that k, `ksr._threshold_at_k(st[m], k)`. Never re-derive a threshold from
  a region's own cells -- a small region would get its own noise-driven cut. State this
  in the function docstring.

  all `m in ms` (logical AND across markers, on the raw frame). Take `sub = d.loc[inter]`.

  across markers, so it cannot express the joint; for a 1-marker config the intersection
  reduces to exactly what it would have returned.
- For each k in `k_values`: `pos[m] = sub[f"{m}_bgsub"].to_numpy(float) >= ksr._threshold_at_k(st[m], k)`.
- For each requested region: mask = `sub["acronym"].to_numpy() == region` (bare-acronym

  `N += mask.sum()`, `f"{m}+" += (pos[m] & mask).sum()`, and when `len(ms) >= 2` and
  `config.emit_double`, `"Double+" += (pos[m0] & pos[m1] & mask).sum()` for the two
  resolved role markers.

**POOL THEN RECOMPUTE.** Thresholds are per-section (each slice gets its own cut at a
