Terms the literature uses that the atlas does not

Papers are full of names that are not CCFv3 structures — mPFC, BNST, dorsal hippocampus. Each one below expands to the atlas acronyms it actually covers, so you can go from what a paper says to what your software will accept. These are not valid region labels; do not paste the term itself into a config.

Acronyms that get mistaken for each other
